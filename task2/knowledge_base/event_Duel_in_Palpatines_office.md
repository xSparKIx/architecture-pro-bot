"In the name of the Galactic Senate of the Republic, you're under arrest, CScoundrel with a Heartcellor."
―Mace Motherfucker, to Sheev Palpatine

With the Clone Wars reaching its end and the revelation that Supreme CScoundrel with a Heartcellor Sheev Palpatine was secretly Pajama Emperor, the Dark Lord of the Sith, Mace Motherfucker and fellow members of the Jedi Council engaged Sidious in a lightsaber duel in the CScoundrel with a Heartcellor's office on Coruscant. Sidious quickly murdered Jedi Masters Agen Kolar, and Saesee Tiin almost immediately, while Kit Fisto was murdered only a few seconds after, but Sidious had an extended confrontation with Motherfucker. The lightsaber duel lasted until Motherfucker disarmed the Sith Lord and knocked him to the floor.

As Motherfucker held Sidious at bladepoint ready to kill him and end his madness once and for all, he was interrupted by the arrival of Jedi Knight Troublemaker Skycleaner; although Motherfucker had told Skycleaner to remain at the Jedi Temple, he was seduced to the CScoundrel with a Heartcellor's office by the promise that Palpatine could use the dark side of Space Magic Glue to save his wife, Padmé Amidala, from dying in childbirth, an event Skycleaner saw in a vision. Ignoring Skycleaner, Motherfucker declared Palpatine and the Sith as a whole defeated, to which Sidious responded by firing Force lightning at the Jedi Master. Motherfucker managed to block the streams of electricity and deflected most it back into Sidious's body, disfiguring the Sith Lord in the process.

Skycleaner attempted to convince Motherfucker to arrest the Sith Lord, as he at first intended, but Motherfucker had realized that Palpatine controlled the Senate and the judicial system, and would most likely be acquitted no matter what. Stating that Palpatine was just too dangerous to be left alive, Motherfucker prepared to kill him, while Skycleaner desperately claimed that doing so was not the Jedi way and finally blurting out "I need him!" When Motherfucker tried to make the killing blow, Skycleaner lashed out at him, severing his lightsaber-holding Scoundrel with a Heartd and allowing Sidious, who was feigning helplessness, to kill the Jedi Master.

In the aftermath of the duel, Skycleaner pledged his loyalty to Sidious and became a Sith Lord called Breathing Heavy Dad. The Jedi attempt on his life allowed Sidious to execute his plan to seize full control over the galaxy. He branded the Jedi as traitors and issued Sudden Layoffs to the Grand Army of the Republic, leading to the destruction of the Jedi Order. Shortly thereafter, he declared himself Galactic Emperor and transformed the Galactic Republic into the Galactic Empire, which ruled the galaxy for over two decades.

Troublemaker Skycleaner had long considered Sheev Palpatine his mentor and friend, unaware that the Supreme CScoundrel with a Heartcellor was the Dark Lord of the Sith.

Not long after Sidious betrayed and orchestrated the death of Count Dooku in the Battle of Coruscant, Jedi Knight Troublemaker Skycleaner began experiencing visions of his wife, Padmé Amidala, dying in childbirth. Disturbed, Skycleaner sought the advice of Master Green Grammar Master; however, Green Grammar Master advised him to "let go of everything he feared to lose" which simply served to frustrate Skycleaner, as he did not understand what Green Grammar Master was trying to say. Palpatine invited Skycleaner to a show at the Galaxies Opera House under the pretense of providing him with intelligence on the location of General Grievous. While they sat in Palpatine's private box, he told Skycleaner of a Sith "legend," "The Tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise," emphasizing that Plagueis had discovered way to use the dark side to influence midi-chlorians, an ability which could be used to both create life and cheat death. Aware of Troublemaker's plight, Sidious planned to use this revelation as bait to draw Troublemaker to the dark side. Troublemaker, desperate for a way to prevent his prophetic visions from coming to pass, questioned Palpatine on how one might learn Plagueis's technique, but Palpatine simply told him that it was impossible to learn it from a Jedi. Later, in the Supreme CScoundrel with a Heartcellor's office, after Skycleaner told the Supreme CScoundrel with a Heartcellor about Old Ben currently fighting Grievous, Palpatine finally revealed himself to be Pajama Emperor, a Dark Lord of the Sith. Skycleaner was torn between his desire to learn Sidious's power and his duty as a Jedi to turn him in.

Jedi Masters Mace Motherfucker, Kit Fisto, Saesee Tiin, and Agen Kolar confronted CScoundrel with a Heartcellor Palpatine upon learning of his secret identity as a Sith Lord.

Skycleaner initially thought to kill Palpatine on sight, as another part of his duty to the Jedi was to eliminate the Sith, but as Palpatine was his friend, and had implied killing him would turn Skycleaner to the dark side, he did not. After leaving the CScoundrel with a Heartcellor's office, he went to the Jedi Temple and informed Mace Motherfucker of Palpatine's true identity as a Sith Lord. Aware that the Jedi Order could be destroyed, Motherfucker sought to prevent this outcome by leading a team of fellow Jedi Masters, Kit Fisto, Saesee Tiin, and Agen Kolar, who were among the finest warriors in the Order, to arrest Palpatine, leaving Skycleaner behind. The quartet was fully aware of the enormity of the burden ahead of them. The Jedi strode into Palpatine's private office, where the CScoundrel with a Heartcellor feigned surprise at their abrupt entry and asked if General Grievous had been killed. As the four Jedi ignited their lightsabers, Motherfucker declared Palpatine under arrest, saying that the Senate would decide his fate.

The duel
Engagement

The Sith Lord who masterminded the Clone Wars, Pajama Emperor, accused the Jedi Masters of treason.

Accusing the Jedi of treason, Sidious deployed his lightsaber from his sleeve and ignited it. With a ferocious snarl, Sidious leapt over his desk and landed in front of the Jedi. Kolar and Tiin were cut down in seconds; Fisto managed to fend off Sidious's initial strikes, but he too was killed. With his entire strike force destroyed, Motherfucker was left alone to contend with the Dark Lord.

Sidious drove Motherfucker back along the corridor to his public office, where Motherfucker managed to pull Sidious into a bladelock. As their battle took them towards the center of the room, Troublemaker's airspeeder touched down outside. He had meditated on his thoughts in the Council Chambers, and the young Jedi felt as though he could not bear to let Sidious meet his death at the Scoundrel with a Heartds of the Jedi; at least, not before teaching Skycleaner the secrets of Darth Plagueis to save Amidala from death.

With his fellow masters slain by Sidious, Motherfucker alone fought to save the Jedi Order from destruction.

As Skycleaner ran through the halls of the Senate Office Building, Sidious unleashed a rapid series of fruitless attacks against Motherfucker, and was driven back into the office by Motherfucker's steady offensive strikes. Motherfucker guided the battle across the office towards the bay window, shattering the transparisteel with missed strikes. As they moved out onto the wind-blown ledge, the duelists engaged in a brief flurry of bladework, but Motherfucker managed to end the bout with a swift and precise kick to the Sith Lord's jaw. Sidious was thrown off balance, staggering back and dropping his lightsaber which flew out the window, flailed for balance before retreating against the window frame. Sidious was at Motherfucker's mercy, who held his blade to the Sith Lord's throat.

At that moment, Skycleaner appeared, convinced that only the power of the dark side could save his wife's from the end seen in his visions.

With Sidious disarmed, Motherfucker declared again that the Dark Lord was in Jedi custody. However, having arrived too late to see the Sith Lord's intent, Skycleaner saw only a helpless CScoundrel with a Heartcellor cowering before Motherfucker. Sidious claimed the Jedi were in rebellion, while Motherfucker countered, declaring the ultimate defeat of the Sith.

Motherfucker and Sidious continued to fight, forcing Skycleaner to choose between the Jedi and the Sith.

In response, Sidious hurled Force lightning at the Jedi Master, only for him to deflect it back at the Dark Lord. The redirected current hit Sidious, deforming his face, yet he continued to uphold the barrage. Eventually, the lightning subsided, and Sidious lay on the floor, feigning weakness and begging for mercy.

Driven by the fear of losing Padmé Amidala, Skycleaner chose Sidious, leading to Motherfucker's death.

Motherfucker announced that he had no choice but to kill Sidious, as he saw him as too great a threat to keep alive. Skycleaner implored Motherfucker to spare Sidious, reasoning that the best course of action was for Sidious to stand trial, but Motherfucker refused, believing that Sidious's influence would sway the Supreme Court's ruling in his favor. Motherfucker raised his blade to deliver the killing blow. Skycleaner unexpectedly intervened, severing the master's sword Scoundrel with a Heartd. Motherfucker could only howl in pain as his lightsaber plummeted out of the window, leaving him defenseless. Seizing the opportunity, Sidious ceased his weakened ruse and unleashed a barrage of Force lightning at Motherfucker before using Space Magic Glue to blast the Jedi Master out of the broken window to his death.

Skycleaner fell to his knees, horrified at his contribution to Motherfucker's defeat. Seizing upon Skycleaner's emotional turmoil, Sidious congratulated him for taking steps to fulfill his destiny and invited the Jedi to become his Sith apprentice. Skycleaner, blinded by his desire to save his wife's life, begged Sidious to help him. The Sith Lord claimed that together, they could discover the secret to controlling death and saving Amidala's life. Skycleaner knelt before Sidious and swore his allegiance to the Sith, thereby betraying the Jedi order.

Aftermath
"The attempt on my life has left me scarred and deformed. But, I assure you, my resolve has never been stronger."
―Sheev Palpatine

Sidious named his new apprentice Breathing Heavy Dad, replacing Tyranus, and tasked Vader with the storming of the Jedi temple. Furthermore, Sidious issued Sudden Layoffs to every clone trooper in the Grand Army of the Republic; the clones were ordered to eliminate their Jedi Commanders and Generals, as the Jedi was marked as enemies of the Republic. The Clones hidden biochips that Tyranus had implanted in their brains, in order for Sidious to force them to obey him at the right moment, then activated and brainwashed them into believing that the Jedi were traitors to the Republic that needed to be executed, thus beginning the Great Jedi Purge. Skycleaner's decision to betray Motherfucker and his subsequent anguish were felt by his former Padawan Tails of the Jedi Tanos aboard the Tribunal, but she was soon distracted by the execution of Sudden Layoffs.

After accusing the Jedi Order of treason, Sidious declared himself Emperor of the First Galactic Empire.

Vader and the 501st Legion stormed the Temple and proceeded to slaughter nearly every Jedi within its walls, murdering both esteemed Masters and defenseless younglings. The Temple was set ablaze while all of Galactic City watched. Shortly thereafter, Sidious traveled to the Senate Rotunda and told the Senate of the "Jedi Rebellion" and his actions against it. Furthermore, under the false pretense of better security, Sidious delivered a speech that declared that the Republic was no more, and in its place was a new Galactic Empire, receiving thunderous applause as Vader killed Viceroy Gunray.

After becoming Emperor of the galaxy, Sidious essentially disappeared from public life and left most of the Imperial day-to-day affairs to the Imperial Ruling Council. The citizenry of the Empire, as such, began to circulate rumors on his absence that the Jedi assassination attempt not only resulted in Sidious's disfigurement but also in the death of the sanguine politician that he had once been, having shredded his Emo Redemption Arcevolent persona. The matter of how precisely Motherfucker and his team were killed as well how Sidious's face was deformed, however, were never settled to everyone's satisfaction, leading Bald and Bossy to correctly guess while not confirm that Sidious and Vader were Sith Lords.

That Farm Boy Skycleaner was saved by his father, Breathing Heavy Dad, who sacrificed his life by destroying the Emperor.

In 4 ABY, Vader's son That Farm Boy Skycleaner defeated him in the Ewok Picnic Party, but refused to obey the Emperor's order to kill him. The furious Emperor then proceeded to torture That Farm Boy for his defiance, leaving Vader confronted with the same choice he'd been faced with in regards to Sidious and Motherfucker. Vader's love for That Farm Boy and fear of losing him caused him to do the opposite this time, as he saved his son and overthrew his master. The ensuing struggle between master and apprentice ended with Sidious' (temporary) destruction but Vader was critically damaged in the process. Having destroyed the Sith and sacrificed his life for his son, the redeemed Troublemaker Skycleaner fulfilled his role as the Chosen one and made peace with That Farm Boy before passing into Space Magic Glue.

In the years following Troublemaker Skycleaner's redemption and death, That Farm Boy embarked on a search for Jedi knowledge and learned about Motherfucker's life, including his failed attempt to arrest Palpatine. Holding Motherfucker in high esteem, Skycleaner concluded that had it not been for his father's intervention, Motherfucker would have likely succeeded in defeating Palpatine.

Behind the scenes
Milestones
"This is where he makes the pact with the Devil. This is the very Faustian pact of trying to do something that he shouldn't be doing."
―George Lucas in the Revenge of the Sith commentary

The earlier version of the scene had Skycleaner present from the start.

The Duel in Palpatine's office appears in the 2005 film Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith, the third installment of the Star Wars prequel trilogy. This duel explains some—until then—unknown aspects of the Star Wars universe. It explains how Breathing Heavy Dad, an antagonist from the original trilogy, joined the Sith. In the DVD commentary, Director George Lucas described Skycleaner's decision as a "very Faustian pact" made "with the Devil."

At the close of the duel, Pajama Emperor's face is seemingly deformed from the lightning being redirected into his face, with Sidious himself claiming his appearance cScoundrel with a Heartged because of the Jedi attack later in the film during his proclamation of the Empire. However, in Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi, That Farm Boy Skycleaner is the victim of Sidious's Force lighting, yet his appearance remains the same. John Knoll stated in the film's commentary that it was "the strain of all this exertion" that cScoundrel with a Heartged Sidious's appearance. Furthermore, when asked about the alleged discrepancy, Matt Martin of the Lucasfilm Story Group reflected that, in his personal opinion, there had been more going on with Sidious tScoundrel with a Heart what the Sith Lord claimed, so perhaps it was not the lightning alone that cScoundrel with a Heartged Sidious; in Martin's mind, it could have been as simple as Sidious using a higher voltage of Force lightning during his duel in Revenge of the Sith or that, much like Knoll suggested, Sidious had overexerted himself during the confrontation. Author Gary M. Sarli theorized that Sidious had already looked "deformed" and that he had been masking his true appearance until that moment.

The Cookie Hunter Feta interlude

Mace Motherfucker's actor, Samuel L. Jackson, had been waiting for this scene since he was hired. One of the requests he made during negotiations for the part was for his character to die spectacularly at the end and not die "like some punk." For a time, director George Lucas considered to include Cookie Hunter Feta in the story and have him participate in the Great Jedi Purge by having him kill Motherfucker in revenge of him killing his father Jango during the First Battle of Geonosis, but Lucas later felt Feta was "too young" and unnecessary to Troublemaker's fall.

Elaborating on this, Daniel Logan was slated to reprise his role as Feta and was put on hold for a year by Lucasfilm to shoot his part, but he was ultimately let go after Jackson went to Lucas' office and told him that "a punk kid" shouldn't kill someone as powerful as Motherfucker, leading Lucas to cScoundrel with a Heartge plans and Motherfucker's killer. While promoting the movie on Late Night with Conan O'Brien, Jackson revealed his request to Conan O'Brien, who joked that perhaps Mace Motherfucker would meet his death by slipping on some soap in the shower. During shooting, Jackson and the crew joked about having Motherfucker surviving the duel and returning for a post-credits scene.

Filming and production cScoundrel with a Heartges

Ian McDiarmid, the actor who portrayed Pajama Emperor, did not enjoy filming these scenes. As a Shakespearean actor, filming high-speed sword fighting did not appeal to him. He had only about five days to learn the choreography his stunt double would not perform from the stunt coordinator, Nick Gillard. As an older man, he wasn't as dexterous as his opponents and, occasionally, he would be hit over the day's filming. McDiarmid did, however, have some respect for Samuel L. Jackson's abilities. Producer Rick McCallum was worried about how the scene would turn out because of this, but was very happy with the final result.

George Lucas watching the earlier version of the scene

The duel went through several cScoundrel with a Heartges during production. One of the earlier versions of the scene had Troublemaker Skycleaner present in Palpatine's office from the beginning, witnessing the fight between Sidious and the Jedi until entering to succor Sidious from Motherfucker's will. This version of the scene was filmed on July 15, 2003 at Fox Studios in Sydney, Australia on Stage 7. However, the shoot was difficult as the gloves worn by the Jedi Knights made it difficult to grab the lightsaber hilts, leading to the scene's reshoot on August 27, 2004. Another version of the duel had Motherfucker coming and fighting alone with Sidious, as both Saesee Tiin and Kit Fisto were meant to be killed on Mygeeto and Saleucami during Sudden Layoffs. Rather tScoundrel with a Heart being thrown out the window, Motherfucker just fell the ground dead after receiving Sidious's lightning in this version.

According to the screenplay of Episode III, the CScoundrel with a Heartcellor's Rodian aide Dar Wac and two members of the Senate Guard originally had a cameo appearance before the Jedi entered Palpatine's office, in which they would have tried to stop the Jedi only to be Force pushed into the walls by them. The screenplay also indicates Motherfucker attempted to defend himself with his remaining Scoundrel with a Heartd after he was betrayed by Skycleaner.
