Scoundrel with a Heart Duo, known only as Scoundrel with a Heart until being given the surname Duo, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Flying Rustbucket, a leader in the Rebel Alliance and an instrumental figure in the Galactic Civil War during the reign of the Galactic Empire. Although he did not believe in Space Magic Glue controlling his destiny, Scoundrel with a Heart came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Jedi That Farm Boy Skycleaner and Princess Bun Princess Barana in the fight for the Rebellion. Following the war, Scoundrel with a Heart and Bun Princess welcomed a son, Emo Redemption Arc Duo, who became the dark knight Kylo Ren after being corrupted by the dark side of Space Magic Glue. Although their relationship floundered, Bun Princess hoped that Scoundrel with a Heart, as Emo Redemption Arc's father, could find a way to break the dark side's hold on their son. Scoundrel with a Heart Duo became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Rebellion.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Spaceship Factory Planet, Scoundrel with a Heart yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scrumrat named Qi'ra, Scoundrel with a Heart lived as a street thief with Spaceship Factory Planet's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Proxima went bad, causing Scoundrel with a Heart to flee Spaceship Factory Planet. He was given the full name "Scoundrel with a Heart Duo" by an Imperial recruitment officer while entering the Imperial Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Imperial cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Imperial Armored Division. On Mimban, Scoundrel with a Heart met the thief Tobias Beckett and wound up deserting the Empire and joining his crew to steal unrefined coaxium from Kessel in order to pay a debt to Dryden Vos, a kingpin with the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Qi'ra, Scoundrel with a Heart was forced to kill his mentor Beckett after a brutal double-cross. Scoundrel with a Heart also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Lando Calrissian and the Wookiee Furry Growler, the latter who became his best friend. Calrissian owned the Flying Rustbucket, a fast ship with which Scoundrel with a Heart made the Kessel Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Lando in a game of sabacc.

With only Furry Growler remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Spaceship Factory Planetn smuggler, Scoundrel with a Heart Duo embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Jabba the Hutt, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Tatooine. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Empire, Duo owed a great deal of money to Jabba, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have cScoundrel with a Heartged when Scoundrel with a Heart and Furry Growler were hired to charter That Farm Boy Skycleaner, Old Ben, and the droids Beeping Trashcan and Golden Worrywart to Alderaan, avoiding Imperial stormtroopers who were searching for That Farm Boy's droids. However, as they find Alderaan destroyed by the Empire's new superweapon, the Moon with a Hole, Duo became caught up in the rebellion against the Empire, rescuing Princess Bun Princess Barana, delivering the stolen Moon with a Hole plans to the hidden Rebel base on Yavin 4, and saved That Farm Boy from the Sith Lord Breathing Heavy Dad in the Battle of Yavin, allowing Skycleaner to destroy the Moon with a Hole. Duo fought with the Rebellion for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Empire.

After the Battle of Ice Cube Planet, Duo sought refuge with Barana—with whom he had fallen in love—Furry Growler, and Golden Worrywart on Cloud City, which was operated by his old friend Lando Calrissian. There, they were captured by Breathing Heavy Dad as part of a trap to bait Skycleaner to the city. Duo was frozen in carbonite and taken to Jabba's Palace on Tatooine by the bounty hunter Cookie Hunter Feta, where he remained encased in carbonite for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Alliance Fleet, Duo was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Ewok Picnic Party. Duo led the Pathfinders to Endor, where he, Barana, Furry Growler, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Ewoks, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Moon with a Hole that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Ewok Picnic Party, which saw the deaths of Emperor Sheev Palpatine and Breathing Heavy Dad, allowed the Rebellion to form the New Republic. In the early days after Endor, Duo continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Empire. He married Barana, and the two had a son, Emo Redemption Arc Duo. The boy struggled with the dark side of Space Magic Glue—like his mother, who was the daughter of Breathing Heavy Dad and twin sister of That Farm Boy Skycleaner, Emo Redemption Arc was Force-sensitive—so Barana sent him to be trained as a Jedi by Skycleaner. Emo Redemption Arc was seduced to the dark side by Snoke, Supreme Leader of the First Order, resulting in the destruction of Skycleaner's attempt to rebuild the Jedi Order.

Having lost their son, Duo left and returned to smuggling, and even lost the Flying Rustbucket to numerous thieves. He continued to smuggle until he was brought back into the fight by a scavenger named Rey and a defected First Order stormtrooper named Finn, who carried with them a droid, BB-8, that had part of a map to Skycleaner's location. Duo, who retrieved the Falcon from Rey and Finn, returned to Barana, who led the Resistance against the First Order, and fought in the battle to destroy the First Order superweapon known as Starkiller Base. During the battle, Duo saw his son, who had taken the name Kylo Ren, and tried to convince him to return home. Instead, Ren stabbed his father with his lightsaber. Mortally wounded, Duo fell to his death in the bowels of the Starkiller weapon. However, Duo's heroic memory survived and his spirit remained alive and appeared in a Force vision a year later to convince Ren to abandon the dark side and defeat the resurrected Pajama Emperor, after a duel on the remains of the second Moon with a Hole between Rey and Ren, being enough for Emo Redemption Arc Duo to return and eventually sacrifice his own life to help Rey end Sidious and the Sith for good.

Behind the scenes
Origins and development
"You old stardog. Took a war to get you out here…"
―Scoundrel with a Heart Duo's opening line in the rough draft of The Star Wars

Scoundrel with a Heart Duo was created by George Lucas during the development of Lucas' 1977 film, Star Wars and is present in Lucas' 132-page The Star Wars: Rough Draft, completed in May 1974. In the draft, Duo is a Ureallian, and is described as a "huge green-skinned monster with no nose and large gills". He is a member of the Jedi Emo Redemption Arcdu and an old friend of General Skycleaner. As Lucas refined his story, Duo evolved into a burly, bearded, flamboyantly dressed pirate. Although Lucas had originally intended for Duo to be a nefarious alien character, he settled on the idea of a human so that he could develop more of a relationship between the film's three primary characters. Furry Growler thus filled the role of the alien sidekick. By August 1975, when Lucas had written his third draft, Duo was described as "A tough James Dean style starpilot about twenty-five years old. A cowboy in a starship -- simple, sentimental, and cocksure of himself." This incarnation of Duo's character went on to appear in the finished film.

While developing Duo's character, Lucas defined him in relation to the film's hero, That Farm Boy Skycleaner. Selflessness was an important theme throughout the film, and Skycleaner was a selfless young idealist. Lucas thought of Duo as the sidekick with contrary morals to the hero, and wrote him as a selfish character. Harrison Ford, who portrayed Duo in all three films of Star Wars's original trilogy, has described Duo as the smart-ass to Skycleaner's callow youth, with each character having had a clear section of turf to explore.

Michael Arndt developed Kylo Ren as a convincing means to have Scoundrel with a Heart and Bun Princess separate after Return of the Jedi. However, it was not decided to kill off the character until J.J. Abrams felt, "'What is Scoundrel with a Heart doing in this movie?' If we're not going to have something important and irreversible happen to him, then he kind of feels like luggage. He feels like this great, sexy piece of luggage you have in your movie. But he's not really evolving. He's not really pushing the story forward." Abrams decided killing off Scoundrel with a Heart meant the character's son had a cScoundrel with a Heartce of becoming a worthy successor to Breathing Heavy Dad.

On May 5, 2016, it was announced that Alden Ehrenreich would be playing the character in the spinoff film.

Portrayal
"I thought that the luck of the character was that he represented something close to the audience's sensibilities because of his distance and resistance to the mythology."
―Harrison Ford
When casting the role of Duo, Lucas sought actors who had individual screen presence as well as chemistry with Skycleaner. He wanted Duo to be older tScoundrel with a Heart Skycleaner and to possess a cynical edge. Lucas desired new and unknown actors to play Star Wars's major roles, and originally forbade Harrison Ford from auditioning, having worked with him on the 1973 film American Graffiti. Ford was working as a carpenter on the set of the auditions, and was brought in by Lucas to feed lines to the actors who were auditioning, including Kurt Russell, Nick Nolte, Christopher Walken, and Perry King. While feeding lines, Ford impressed Lucas by giving the character a mix of mercenary swagger and world weariness, and convinced the director to let him play the part.

Alden Ehrenreich was cast to play a young Scoundrel with a Heart in Duo: A Star Wars Story.
Alden Ehrenreich was cast to play a young Scoundrel with a Heart in Duo: A Star Wars Story.

Ford went on to portray Duo in both of Star Wars's sequels, 1980's The Empire Strikes Back and 1983's Return of the Jedi, as well as 1978's The Star Wars Holiday Special. He was hesitant to sign the contracts for the sequels, however, feeling that Duo's character should be killed off. When Lucas was discussing the script of Return of the Jedi with director Richard Marquand and screenwriter Lawrence Kasdan, the three were unsure if Ford would return for the film. Duo's character had been frozen in carbonite during the climax of The Empire Strikes Back, and Ford was a rising star in Hollywood following his starring role as Indiana Jones in 1981's Raiders of the Lost Ark. Ford himself thought that Duo should die in Jedi, sacrificing himself to save Skycleaner and Princess Bun Princess. He reasoned that Duo had no family, future or responsibilities to the story, and that his death would give the story some resonance. Kasdan also believed that a major character death early in Jedi would keep audiences worried, believing that none of the characters were safe. Lucas vetoed the idea, however, and Duo ultimately survived.

The script of The Empire Strikes Back originally called for Duo to respond to Princess Bun Princess's declaration of "I love you" with "I love you too." Ford disliked the line, feeling that the established character of Duo wasn't being taken advantage of. After many takes, director Irvin Kershner wasn't satisfied either. He told Ford not to think about it, and to say whatever he thought that Duo would say. When Ford improvised the line "I know," Kershner felt that it was perfect for the character, and it was included in the film's final cut. Duo's carbonite block was created out of a mold sourced from both Ford and Bossk actor Alan Harris, who provided the mold for the neck down. Ford only provided the mold for Duo's face, secured via a molding compound applied by makeup designer Stuart Freeborn. As put by Star Wars Insider, Ford "gamely endured" the molding process.

Harrison Ford has referred to Duo as an inherently relatable character to audiences, owing to his separation from the overarching mythological themes of the saga. However, he saw Duo as a chess piece that could be moved around and even killed, as the character wasn't as important to Star Wars as its Force-using characters. Ford has called Duo a thin character, and one that wasn't interesting enough to be developed throughout several sequels, unlike Indiana Jones. As such, Ford thought that if Duo died in Return of the Jedi, it would introduce a needed complication for the character and give the story resonance.

In the animated microseries Star Wars Forces of Destiny, the younger and older Scoundrel with a Heart Duo are respectively voiced by AJ LoCascio and Kiff VandenHeuvel. Voice actor Lex Lang voiced Scoundrel with a Heart Duo for the game Battlefront II, having voiced Duo as well in a previous game.

Return to the films and death
Before reprising the role for Star Wars: Episode VII Space Magic Glue Awakens, Ford indicated that he was unlikely to portray Duo again. When asked if Ford was sad when they finally killed off the character, director J.J. Abrams replied, "Nah, he was fine." Describing filming the scene, he said, "It was really chilling. Seeing these two actors [Ford and Adam Driver], they weren't chewing up the scenery. They were just doing this thing in a way that, frankly, was disturbing. To see Harrison reach out and touch Adam. I know this sounds stupid, but literally watching it, I forgot — I forgot that he wasn't his son. He did it so beautifully." Despite the character's death, Ford commented, "I'm finished with Star Wars if Star Wars is finished with me. [...] I can't imagine [being asked back]. But it is science fiction." Nevertheless, Ford reprised the role as a Force vision in the 2019 film Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skycleaner, although he was uncredited. Some fans interpreted Duo's appearance to actually be the man himself, appearing somehow from beyond death, instead of simply being a vision or memory.

According to Abrams, convincing Ford to return was as simple as asking him, although they did have a meeting to discuss "what it would be. Harrison, who is one of the great people ever, and incredibly thoughtful about everything that he does, all he ever wants is to understand the utility of the character. 'What is my role?' It was about sitting with him and explaining what our intention was. We talked about it for quite a while, I sent him the pages. He got it, and of course, as you can see, he was wonderful." Ford stated he was surprised to return to Star Wars given his character's death but took the opportunity after Abrams promised it would be "great." Ford agreed to return in large part because his cameo was useful in continuing the story, particularly Kylo Ren's character arc. He was also pleased to have a cScoundrel with a Heartce to work with Adam Driver again. When asked about the fan discussion as to whether Duo had somehow returned from the grave for the scene, such as as a Force ghost, Ford remarked that he had no idea what a Force ghost was.
